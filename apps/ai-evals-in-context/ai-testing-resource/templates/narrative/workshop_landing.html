Five Stages

1

Foundation

Build a RAG pipeline, add generic quality metrics, then encode domain expertise into custom metrics.

2

Acceptance

Curate golden datasets with positive and negative examples that define what "good" looks like.

3

Validation

Measure inter-rater reliability with Cohen's and Fleiss' kappa. Calibrate your team.

4

Improve

Run dual improvement loops—refine the pipeline and the judge in parallel, with regression tests.

5

Reporting

Multi-turn conversations, trace validation, and a Test Summary Report wired into CI/CD.
