## Design Context

### Users
People who see AI changing their world and choose agency over displacement. They may be in any field — operations, analysis, project management, creative work, or anything else. They are not becoming "a developer" — they are becoming someone who can discover problems, prototype solutions, advocate for resources, and ship. The portal serves one persona across four levels of development: Curiosity, Clarity, Capability, and Consistency.

### Brand Personality
Professional, warm, substantive. Not whimsical, not sterile, not startup trendy. The tone is that of a knowledgeable colleague who respects your intelligence. Three words: grounded, capable, inviting.

### Aesthetic Direction
Space exploration metaphor grounded in habitation — not abstract sci-fi, but discovery with purpose. Terrain over stars. Instruments over weapons. Basecamp as community. Warm earth tones (regolith, sediment) for content surfaces, deep space navy for navigation chrome. System font stack only. Flat vector illustrations with the brand palette. No gradients, no drop shadows, no glassmorphism.

Color palette:
- Deep Space (#0F1B2D) — headers, navigation, authority
- Orbital Blue (#1E3A5F) — secondary dark
- Instrument Blue (#3B82C4) — interactive elements, links, active states
- Signal Orange (#D4763A) — accent, CTAs, important callouts
- Atmosphere Teal (#2A9D8F) — success, growth, progress
- Regolith (#F4F1EC) — main background
- Shelter White (#FAFAF8) — card surfaces
- Sediment (#E8E3DA) — secondary backgrounds
- Dust (#8B8178) — muted text

### Design Principles
1. Show before tell — demonstrate through real work before explaining
2. Scaffolding, not levels — no beginner/intermediate/advanced labels
3. Instruments, not magic — tools increase capability, not dependency
4. Community is the curriculum — peer work is core content
5. Private reflection, public portfolio — self-assessment private, completed work shareable
